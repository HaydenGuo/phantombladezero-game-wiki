Plan the Phantom Blade Zero platinum around missable Tales first, the fullest ending route second and equipment cleanup last. The trophy roadmaps list 36 trophies, no online requirement and a possible single planned playthrough on any difficulty. Use separate manual saves at region boundaries. A collectible can often wait; a Tale that fails after a story event needs an earlier save. This difference should determine the order of your run. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={2} />

## Phase 1: protect the early missable Tales

| Before advancing | Complete or check | Trophy route |
|---|---|---|
| Leaving Weeping Creek Valley | First available A Severed Mindseal objective | Spectral Blade |
| Defeating Jing Ye | Hand in Apprentice Healer's three herbs | The Healer's Legacy |
| Defeating Bai Chilian | Flesh Lily delivery, A Healer's Mercy and Lanlan's Baishan Tale acceptance | Healer and Baishan trophies |
| Entering Pangzhen from Village of Lu | Accept The Journey Home and Young Master Lan; finish their initial objectives | Together Till the End and A Wanderer's Honor |
| Defeating Yubi after Wu Lengshuang | Interact with the body in flowers beyond The Confinement | The Last of Baishan |

A Severed Mindseal has an uncertain failure trigger, so do its available objective as soon as it appears and retain the Valley save. For Yu'er, collect the lilies from Hua's arena, deliver them and keep the returned ordinary flower. Hand in the herbs before Jing Ye, then complete the Forest healer conversation before Bai Chilian. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={3} /> <Citation source={2} />

For Baishan, speak with Lanlan near the Thieves' Camp bridge before the boss deadline. Follow the Cliffside Lodge crank-and-cellar stage. Later, after Wu Lengshuang in Mandate Encampment, interact with the body in the flowers before defeating Yubi. Checking that interaction immediately is more dependable than assuming the Tale will complete automatically with the region boss. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={2} />

## Phase 2: finish the lake investigation

Secrets at the Heart of the Lake connects several optional areas. Obtain the four acolyte masks from Yanru in Village of Lu, Lei Ting in Pangzhen, Guhe in Meigu Lake and Hansui in Crimson Manor. The Manor depends on the Herald portal and Puppet's Tongue progression, so work through those prerequisites as the relevant regions open. <Citation source={4} /> <Citation source={5} />

Use the masks at the mirror in northwestern Meigu Lake. Clear the hidden realm, defeat its boss and place the Yin and Yang eyes in the resulting puppet statue. Finish the ritual encounter by defeating the puppet monks. This advances the lake Tale and its trophy, while supplying part of the fuller ending route. <Citation source={4} /> <Citation source={5} />

## Phase 3: five pillars and the later puppet fight

Complete Bloodline Awakening's five Lóng Pillar challenges in the Valley, Crimson Forest, eastern Meigu Lake, the Phantom Illusionist realm and Mirage Estate. The last pillar is inside the Estate, so a blanket instruction to finish every pillar before entering it would be impossible. Finish the earlier-region prerequisites, save at the transition and complete the Estate pillar when available. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={4} />

For The Four Freed, track Yuzan, Yubi, Yuding and Yucong. The later Yucong encounter belongs to Triple Threat in the frozen Forest; prepare Blazing Edge, the tempered Sanguine Reach. Keep a save before the Mirage Estate transition and another at The Last Chance before the finale, because reports differ on recovery windows for some unfinished objectives. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={2} />

## Phase 4: optional encounters and collection thresholds

| Checklist | Required target |
|---|---|
| Main weapon level | One at level 30 |
| Phantom Edge level | One at level 30 |
| Soul Tempering | Ten main weapons and ten Phantom Edges |
| Memory Flowers | Ten complete flowers, including their required petals |
| Accessories | Thirty collected |
| Banyan challenges | Five completed |
| Quick Hand's requests | Complete all sets and hand them in |

Keep Quick Hand's requests on the platinum checklist because Lost and Found requires them. Their necessity for the true ending remains disputed, which does not change that trophy requirement. Check complete flowers rather than individual petals and count distinct tempered items rather than repeated ordinary upgrades. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={2} />

Before starting New Game+, compare your trophy list with the unfinished encounters and collection targets. Finish recoverable cleanup in the current world. Reload an earlier manual save for an already failed missable Tale. The order above protects the highest-risk steps; complete item-by-item collectible routes and every supporting Tale still require more detailed coverage than this roadmap provides.
