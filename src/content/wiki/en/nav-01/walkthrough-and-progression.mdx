Before pushing through Crimson Forest, finish Yu'er's Apprentice Healer stage in the Valley, then work through the Thieves' Camp conversations and Forest side quests before the region's major boss transitions. The Forest Outpost Courtyard Key opens the Red Serpent House route; the similarly named Backstreet Courtyard Key belongs to a later Pangzhen objective. Keeping the quest order and key names separate prevents unnecessary travel and protects the healer chain. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={2} />

## Before Jing Ye: finish the Valley healer stage

Apprentice Healer asks for Blood-Stopper Vine, Congealeaf and Blemish Goldweed. Collect them in Weeping Creek Valley and hand them to Yu'er before defeating Jing Ye. That deadline makes it worth checking the Tale while you can still complete the early step, rather than assuming you can return after every story event. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={3} />

Use the Holding Cell area for Blood-Stopper Vine, the Rusted Loong Key route for Congealeaf, and the Valley Lóng Pillar area for Blemish Goldweed. After collecting the set, speak to Yu'er and confirm the journal advances. Possession and hand-in are separate checks; carrying the herbs into the next boss does not complete the stage. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={3} />

## Thieves' Camp conversations

At the camp, work through the NPC conversations before continuing along the main route. Yu'er offers A Healer's Mercy, and the Gentle Thief's questions close Honor Among Thieves when answered correctly. The answers correspond to the stolen jade belt, the butterfly and the child's unreported fate. <Citation source={2} /> <Citation source={4} />

| Question's subject | Answer to select |
|---|---|
| What was stolen | The patron or landowner's jade belt |
| The relevant creature | Butterfly |
| What happened to the child | The letter did not say; the fate is unknown |

The exact English phrasing can vary, so follow the answer's meaning. Correct answers award Silent Wrap and complete that conversation's objective. The region guide marks this reward as missable after the main Forest boss, which makes an early camp stop useful even if your immediate aim is story progression. <Citation source={2} /> <Citation source={4} />

## Forest Outpost Courtyard Key and Red Serpent House

Follow the cave route to the outpost buildings, then search the building reached after exiting the cave and going upstairs. Collect the Forest Outpost Courtyard Key and use it on the locked courtyard gate. This gives access toward the Red Serpent House bell. Search the adjacent buildings as you go, because the route description and nearby chest can otherwise make it easy to inspect only one room. <Citation source={2} /> <Citation source={4} />

If the lock names a Backstreet Courtyard Key, you are following a different objective. That later key comes from Chen Zhu's Ledger of Atonement branch in Mountain of Imprisonment and opens a Pangzhen courtyard. Read the inventory's full item name before deciding the Forest key has failed. <Citation source={5} /> <Citation source={6} />

## Before Bai Chilian: check the open Tales

Complete A Healer's Mercy and accept Baishan's next stage before the main Forest boss. Also review Honor Among Thieves and any open Valley investigation that lists a Forest deadline. Return to the relevant NPC and confirm the journal's completed state before advancing. A manual save before a major boss provides a recovery point if the next event changes an unfinished Tale. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={3} />

These checks serve a different purpose from finding every collectible. Prioritize timed conversations first, then use your remaining exploration time on chests and optional paths. You can track completion more clearly when the journal's quest state and the map's collectible percentage are treated as separate records.

## Returning to the frozen Forest

Entering Mirage Estate changes parts of Crimson Forest to snow and ice. Previously inaccessible frozen routes become relevant, and four bells in the changed area need to be collected again for area completion. A missing bell count after the transition can therefore reflect the new regional state. <Citation source={2} /> <Citation source={3} />

Triple Threat also returns to the Forest later. Prepare the Blazing Edge and follow the updated Tale toward the Three-Faced Puppet encounter. Complete the early conversations during your first visit, keep a save before world-changing progression, then use the frozen revisit for objectives that specifically require that later state. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={3} />
