## Flesh Lily Fields below the Valley tower

Travel to Valley Sha-Chi Tower 1st Floor. Use the stairs and route around the tower's side to reach the long descending wooden ramp. Follow it down toward Flesh Lily Fields and Hua's arena. After defeating Hua, inspect the corpse by the pavilion and collect the Twinned Flesh Lilies. The weapon reward and the quest pickup are separate items. <Citation source={4} /> <Citation source={5} />

Return to the outpost for Yu'er near Forge after Peng Meng. If you are searching near the tower but never descending, reset at the named bell and follow the downward route. If Hua is already defeated but the Tale remains open, check the pavilion pickup and your inventory before searching for another boss.

## Two courtyard keys with different destinations

The Forest Outpost Courtyard Key is found in the Forest outpost buildings after the cave route. It opens the gate toward Red Serpent House. Search the room reached upstairs after leaving the cave, then return to the nearby courtyard gate. <Citation source={1} /> <Citation source={6} />

The Backstreet Courtyard Key belongs to Chen Zhu's later quest branch. In Mountain of Imprisonment, return his Ledger of Atonement and follow the challenge/combat branch to defeat him. Take that key back to the Pangzhen courtyard. A Forest search cannot complete this later acquisition route because the quest and region are different. <Citation source={2} /> <Citation source={7} />

## Entering Crimson Manor

Crimson Manor's entrance is below the Valley Sha-Chi Tower, but access depends on progress elsewhere. Complete the three Herald's Illusion portal encounters associated with Village of Lu, Mandate Encampment and Mountain of Imprisonment. Then return to the hanging puppet conversation for the Puppet's Tongue and the related eye requirement used at the entrance. <Citation source={8} /> <Citation source={9} />

When the door still refuses entry, check the prerequisite sequence in your journal. Reaching the correct physical door is only the navigation half of the task. Complete the outstanding portal or puppet interaction, then return to the Valley entrance with the required items.

## Illusory Pipa and the Meigu Lake barrier

The instrument is repaired from parts obtained across the journey: the head through Dusk's Village of Lu stage, the backplate after Mandate progression, and the strings through Yan Qiansui near Meigu Lake's sunken opera route. Bring the completed set to Myst. The repaired Pipa is used for the barrier in northeastern Meigu Lake toward Mountain of Imprisonment. <Citation source={10} /> <Citation source={11} />

Check the repaired item's inventory state before returning to the barrier. Having several components is an intermediate step; the repair interaction completes the usable instrument. Likewise, a quest marker for a later location may require story progress before the route opens.

## A quick method for any blocked route

Read the objective's exact name, locate the nearest named bell and identify whether the obstacle asks for an item, conversation or world-state change. Check those conditions before sweeping the map again. Keep a manual save before major transitions and note whether the region is in its original or frozen state. This gives each return trip a specific purpose and makes similar-looking doors much easier to distinguish.
